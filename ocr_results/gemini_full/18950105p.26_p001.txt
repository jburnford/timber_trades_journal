FAILURES AND ARRANGEMENTS.
[It must not be over-looked that
creditors whose names are not published
hold security for their debts, and expect to be paid in
full amount.]
CARTER, C. T., builder, Willesden,
Willesden, N.W.-The first meeting of creditors
under this failure was held on the 2nd inst. before
Mr. E. S. Grey, Assistant Official Receiver.
Accounts showed unsecured liabilities £2,109 11s. 11d.,
and debts fully secured £1,100. The gross
assets consisting of book debts £283 13s. 5d. from the
debtor's separate estate, were estimated to produce
£283 13s. 5d. from the separate estate.
Creditors :-
Beeson Bros., Harlesden
Clydesdale Iron Foundry, Harlesden
Thames Street, E C
Lines, R., Kensington
Marchant, M., Harlesden
Palfreman, Foster, & Co., Great Eastern
Street, E.C.
Rasey, H., Kilburn
Smith & Co., East Hill, S.W.
Shaw, T. F., Harlesden
Williams & Wallington, Harlesden
(estimated)
Watts, E., timber merchant, Harlesden
The debtor states that he commenced business
about 1884 with a capital of £100, and from two
years prior to 1889 he was in partnership
with another person. He attributes his insol-
vency chiefly to losses on contracts and to
building speculations in which he engaged at Har-
lesden. Mr. Street was appointed trustee on
behalf of creditors. The debtor submitted a pro-
posal, and his affairs were left in his hands in
bankruptcy, the Official Receiver remaining
trustee.
