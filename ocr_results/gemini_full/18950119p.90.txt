FAILURES AND ARRANGEMENTS.
[It must not be overlooked that in many cases creditors whose names are published in these lists hold security for their debts, sometimes for the full amount.]

BACON, DAVID HENRY, builder, 3, Merton Road, and 4, Gladstone Road, Wimbledon.-The first meeting of the creditors interested in this estate will take place on Friday at the offices of the Official Receiver, Railway Approach, London Bridge, S.E. The statement of affairs disclosed gross liabilities amounting to £1,955, of which £455 17s. 2d. was due to unsecured creditors, to fully secured creditors £1,500, the value of the securities being estimated at £2,200, thus leaving a surplus asset to partly secured creditors, £45, the value of the securities being estimated at £20, thus leaving a balance of £25 to rank against the estate for dividend; total liabilities expected to rank against the estate for dividend, £480 17s. 2d. The assets were returned at £883 11s. 2d., from which deducted preference claims amounting to 10s. have to be £883 1s. 2d. The estimated nett assets at £838 1s. 2d. The debtor said he filed his petition in order to gain time and protect the estate, but he did not consider himself insolvent. The receiving order was made on the debtor's own petition, and an order of adjudication has also been made. The debtor commenced business as a builder at Wimbledon in October, 1892, with a capital of £100. After a long discussion it was decided to adjourn the debtor might be able to make an order that the debtor might be able to put forward a proposal for composition.

BARRY, DAVID, builder and contractor, Ratcliffe-on-Trent.-The public examination of this debtor was appointed to be held yesterday week at the Nottingham Bankruptcy Court, before Mr. Registrar Speed, but when his name was called there was no response. Dr. Truman asked that the examination might be adjourned in order that they might make an effort to secure the debtor's attendance on another occasion. The Registrar granted the application of the Official Receiver.

BETTS, WATERMAN, builder, Kingston-on-Thames.-This bankrupt applied to Judge Vernon Lushington, Q.C., at the Kingston Bankruptcy Court, on Friday, for his order of discharge. Mr. T. W. J. Britten opposed for the Board of Trade. The Official Receiver's report stated that this was the debtor's second failure. He started business in 1870 without any capital, and proceeded to build houses as a speculation. He failed with liabilities amounting to £2,000, and no dividend was paid. After twelve months he again commenced building, having a capital of £72, and he erected houses at prices ranging from £750 to £1,000 each. This time he failed with liabilities £1,200, and not sufficient assets to pay the costs. No books of account had been kept. The debtor had contracted debts without reasonable expectation of being able to pay them, and had brought on his bankruptcy by rash and hazardous speculation. The discharge would be refused.

BLACHFORD, HENRY, builder, Southcote Road, Bournemouth.-The first meeting of the creditors herein has been held before the Official Receiver of Salisbury, at Bournemouth, when the debtor submitted a statement of affairs showing gross liabilities of £43,040 2s. 6d. and deficiency nil. The liabilities to rank were £18,173 16s. 8d. and the gross assets (consisting of surplus from secured creditors) were returned at the same amount. The debtor alleged his failure to be due to "heavy losses," and the general depression in trade, and inability to sell or let properties. The debtor commenced business 23 years ago, with a capital of £500. He had incurred heavy losses in connection with an hotel. The Official Receiver is of opinion that, having regard to the present state of the building trade in Bournemouth, it will be a difficult matter to dispose of the properties at a fair value. Mr. A. Blachford, of Cowes, was appointed trustee in bankruptcy, with a committee of inspection.

COUSINS, ARCHIBALD, saw-mill furnisher, 312, Old Street, E.C.-The first meeting of creditors under this failure was held on Tuesday, before Mr. G. W. Chapman, Official Receiver. The liabilities were returned at £1,541 11s. 5d., and the assets at £713 6s. 8d. It appeared from the debtor's statement that he commenced business in November, 1893, with £300 capital, borrowed of his friends. The business consisted, only a few of the smaller orders, wood turners, and others were manufactured, especially adapted for their work, by himself, the rest of the work being put out to another person to be manufactured, the debtor simply supplying the materials. He had great working expenses, and had for some time been in difficulties, but he had for some time been in hopes of receiving a sum of £10,000 from the Powers of creditors.
