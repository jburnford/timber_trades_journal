BROCK, W. & F. W., trading as Brock & Son, contractors, St. Philip's Marsh.--At the Bristol Bankruptcy Court yesterday week, Mr. Press appeared for Mr. E. T. Collins, trustee; Mr. W. H. Brown for Mr. W. Brock, and Mr. Pillers for Mr. F. W. Brock. Mr. Press said at the last examination Mr. F. W. Brock undertook to file an account of accommodation bill transactions, and the accounts filed were not what was asked for. The Official Receiver said it was most important that further information should be furnished.
