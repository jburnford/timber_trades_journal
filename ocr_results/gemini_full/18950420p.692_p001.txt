FAILURES AND ARRANGEMENTS.
[It must not be overlooked that in many cases creditors whose names are published in these lists hold security for their debts, sometimes for the full amount.]

FENN, WILLIAM, joiner and builder, 27, Sheep Street, Northampton.—The public examination was examined in the Northampton County Court on the 11th inst. The liabilities were £208 18s. 10d., and the assets £10 10s. In answer to the Official Receiver's questions, the debtor stated that he commenced business in 1888 with a capital of £10, which he had saved. He commenced to speculate in land. He got on very well for a time, and then he lost money. He attributed his failure to bad debts, to competition, and to his speculation in land. He had kept no books of account. The examination was adjourned.
