LANGDON DANIEL WILSON.
Of Maidstone, Tonbridge, and Sevenoaks, applied at the Maidstone County Court on Tuesday week for his discharge from bankruptcy. Mr. J. J. Ellis appeared for the applicant, and Mr. E. M. Smith for creditors. The liabilities were £19,000, and the assets were estimated to realise £7,000, the dividend being 7s. in the pound. The Judge suspended debtor's discharge for four years.
SCOTT, JOHN,
builder, Dalston, formerly of Hesket New Market.—This debtor was examined on Tuesday for public examination before Judge Stevenson, at the last sitting of the Carlisle County Court. The Official Receiver said he was not satisfied with the examination he had had, and he informed his Honour because he had reason for believing that the debtor had not disclosed the whole of his property. The debtor was in reply to the Official Receiver admitted that on the night of the receiving order being granted he was in a public-house in Dalston, and that there was a man there named Bell, but he denied having made a bargain with him about a pigeon match. The Official Receiver said he was not at all satisfied with the answers given by the debtor, as he felt sure that there were some assets somewhere; therefore he asked his Honour to make an order adjourning the examination sine die. His Honour made the order accordingly, and he informed the debtor that he would have to make a complete disclosure of his assets.
THRUSBY, THOMAS, of Kants, managing Director of Throstle Nest Mills, ford, late Manchester.—The Manchester Official Receiver reports that the liabilities in this case are £36,574, and assets are estimated to produce £31,504. The debtor attributes the failure to the failure of Robert Burden, of Chester, for whom he had accepted and endorsed bills of £10,000, and to his own insolvency. The Official Receiver said he had no questions to ask, and his Honour made an order for the closure of his examination.
