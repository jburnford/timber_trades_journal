AUCTION MART AT THORNTON.—The new auction mart erected by John Swan & Sons, Limited, in close proximity to Thornton Junction, Fifeshire, has been opened. The mart has been built by Mr. Waddell, Burntisland, from plans prepared by Mr. Storrar, architect, Cupar. It has a frontage of 80 ft. by 49 ft.
IRVINE DEAN OF GUILD COURT. — At this Court, Dean of Guild Jaffray pre-siding, Mr. Connor, Bridgegate, had plans for two blocks of two-story houses with attics in Bridgegate passed, on condition that the dividing gable be 2 ft. 3 in. in place of 2 ft. thick. Plans for two cottages at Irvine harbour, submitted on behalf of Mr. Curle, were also passed.
BOYCOTTING THE NORTH BRITISH RAIL-WAY.—The Kirkcaldy Chamber of Commerce has issued to the traders in the district a circular, asking them to support their reso-lution to divert their traffic from the North British Railway. Many of the principal manufacturers have allowed their names to be appended to the circular, and the work of diverting the traffic is being actively carried out.
NEW FREESTONE QUARRY.—Mr. William Brown, Burngreen, has opened a new free-stone quarry on the Kilsyth and Colzium estate of Sir Archibald Edmonstone, Bart., of Duntreath. The stone, so far as it has been tested, is of uniform colour, very white in appearance, and resembles the Bannock-burn and Bishopbriggs stone. It is also considered to be a very durable weather stone, and from the bores that have been put down on the estate it is computed that there are from 200 to 300 acres of rock, varying from 70 to 100. ft. in thickness, which can be easily worked.
FOREST FIRE IN PERTHSHIRE. — Last week John Hughes, a tramp labourer, 60 years of age, was brought before Hon. Sheriff-Substitute Wilson at Perth Sheriff Court, where he emitted a declaration on a charge of having wilfully set fire to a plan-tation at the head of Loch Rannoch. The plantation consists of several acres, and the whole has been destroyed, the fire, it is supposed, having commenced at the end of the week. On account of the inaccessible nature of the district it was some time before the matter was reported to the County Police, but in the meantime the Inverness-shire police arrested Hughes at Fort-William on suspicion. Accused has been handed over to the Perthshire police. The plantation is on the estate of Sir Robert Menzies, Bart.
