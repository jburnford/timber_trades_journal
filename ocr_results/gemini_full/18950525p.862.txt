TRADE AT PAISLEY.—At the fortnightly meeting of the Trades’ Council the various delegates reported on the state of trade in town as follows:—Engineers, moderate; plumbers, busy; joiners, fair; saw-millers, fair; sailors, very busy; woodturners, fair; masons, very good.

FIRE AT BO’NESS.—A disastrous fire occurred at one of Messrs. Love & Stewart’s Storage Yards, at Bo’ness, on the evening of Thursday week, at the yard opposite the passenger station there. The firm are happy to say, however, that the event will not interfere in the least with their ability to execute all their orders entrusted to them, their other stocks at their Grangepans Saw-mill (where the whole of the sawing is done, and at the harbour and dock, as well as at South Alloa and other ports, affording them ample facility for the prompt and efficient fulfilment of all requirements.
