AMERICAN NOTES.
Messrs. A. S. Kibbee & Son, of Albany, N.Y. have sold 500,000 ft. of high grade Michigan pine to the People's Line for a new steamer. They also sold 500,000 feet of lower grade pine to eastern parties, and through their New York office 500,000 sleepers to go to Para, South America.
The war in Cuba continues to have a baleful influence over Mobile's export trade of lumber. Timber shipments to the United Kingdom and Continent show a healthy increase over those of the same time last year, though the demand for hewn timber is light. Sawn timber commands better prices, selling on a basis of the same for choice lots averaging above this figure.

PUBLIC WORKS AT ANTWERP.—The Secretary of State for Foreign Affairs has received from Her Majesty's Consul-General at Antwerp a despatch reporting that contracts for extensive railway works are about to be allotted by the Belgian railway authorities. The cost of the works, the contract for which will be allotted on July 3rd, will exceed 2,000,000 francs. Towards the middle of August a further contract, involving the expenditure of some 5,000,000 francs, will be allotted for the construction of a new railway station. Further details as to conditions of tender have been asked for, and when received can be seen at the Commercial Department of the Foreign Office, between the hours of 11 and 6.
