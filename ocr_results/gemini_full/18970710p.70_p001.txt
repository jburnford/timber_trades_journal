GREENOCK.
The first cargo of the season from Quebec arrived this week per "Lammersmoor" (s) to Singleton, Dunn, & Co. Particulars are 100 logs elm, 203 logs birch, 71 logs square pine, 1,605 logs waney boardwood, 134 logs red pine, 4,284 pieces prime deals, 9,817 pine ends, 2,102 spruce deals. Other arrivals are the "Christiania" (s) from Pensacola, with 94 logs poplar, 1,835 logs pitch pine, 246 pieces pitch pine deals, and 90 logs hewn, to account of Allison, Cousland, & Co.; and the "Gaa Paa" (645), from Bankok, brings for same consignees 844 pieces teak squares, 306 pieces teak planks.
The above are all discharging in the James Watt Dock.

LEITH.
The arrivals this week are not so numerous, consisting of 33,299 staves and 1,647 planks per "Buda" (s) from Libau, 1,558 logs pitch pine and 687 deals per "Casten Boe" from Mobile, 15,084 battens per "Fremat" from Hernosand, 7,590 battens per "Catherine" from Soderhamn, 9,581 battens per "Otto" from Sundswall, 13 pcs. oak per "Rona" (s), from Copenhagen, and 33,359 deals and battens, per "Peru" from Hernosand. The pitch pine cargo is for Garland & Roger, and seems an excellent shipment of this useful timber. The docks are still very busy, the recent arrivals being still in process of removal, but the consumptive demand is not quite so large as could be wished, and there seems to be more goods storing than usual at this season. The firm position of prices and increased cost of importation prevents merchants making any concession to buyers, and there is possibly no inducement offered to anticipate requirements by purchasing in advance.
