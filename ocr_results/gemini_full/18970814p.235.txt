Mr. D. W. Beattie held a public sale on Tuesday, 10th inst., at the Edinburgh Dock.
Under the circumstances referred to above, a large attendance of buyers and an active demand was scarcely to be expected, but a fair quantity of the various descriptions was disposed of, including American yellow pine deals and boards, as well as Baltic deals and battens.
For all the better quantities prices were firmly maintained, although some inferior parcels were sold cheap.
