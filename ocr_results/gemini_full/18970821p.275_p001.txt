LEITH.
The arrivals continue on a moderate scale, but included in the list are some important shipments, viz., 14,098 battens per "Lilly," from Sundswall; 15,077 deals and battens per "Zephyr," from Cronstadt; 44,009 deals and battens, per "Moscow" (s), from St. Petersburg; 29,309 deals and ends and 132 pine logs per "Fremona" (s), from Montreal; 25,434 deals and battens per "Bertha," from Abo; 22,267 battens per "Vulcan," from Hudikswall.
Low, Kinghorn, & Co. held a public sale at the Burgh Saw-mills, on Tuesday, 17th inst.
The goods offered, included, besides floorings, linings, and sarkings, American yellow pine deals and boards, as well as Baltic red and white pine deals and battens.
There was a good average attendance of buyers, but only a moderate demand was experienced.
For what was sold, however, fair prices were obtained, and a good proportion of the various descriptions was placed.
The pronounced rise in freights from the Baltic is making merchants realise the necessity of advancing prices in order to cover the increased cost of importation, and there appears to be a general disposition towards increased firmness.
The one untoward influence is the masons' strike, which still continues locally, and if persisted in may, it is said, develop some such action on the part of employers in other districts as has been seen in the case of the engineering industry.
It is sincerely to be hoped, however, that this may be avoided.
