FAILURES AND ARRANGEMENTS.
[It must not be overlooked that in many cases creditors whose names are published in these lists hold security for their debts, sometimes for the full amount.]
ASHWORTH, JOHN, builder, Maza Cottage, Davyhulme, Manchester, formerly carrying on business at King Street and Jolton Street, Manchester.—The statutory meeting of the creditors interested under this failure took place on Tuesday at the offices of the Official Receiver, Manchester. The statement of affairs filed by the Official Receiver showed liabilities amounting to £1,991 3s. 6d., and assets estimated to produce £696 1s. 6d., thus leaving a deficiency of £1,295 2s. It appeared from the report and observations of the Official Receiver that the bankrupt commenced business twelve years ago with a capital of £100, and he alleged his failure to have been caused through depreciation in the value of house property. The debtor, in reply to questions, admitted that he had not kept proper books of account, and as a rule had only kept memoranda. Eventually it was decided to appoint Mr. Townley Trotter, Chartered Accountant, as trustee, to act in conjunction with a committee of inspection, which was also appointed.
EVERALL, W. R. & R. E., builders, Swan Hill, Shrewsbury.—The public examination of the above-named debtor took place at the Shire Hall, Shrewsbury, on Tuesday, before the Registrar. The Official Receiver stated that according to the debtors' statement of affairs the assets were estimated to produce more than the liabilities, but he did not think the book debts would realise the amount they were returned at. The debtors alleged the failure to have been caused through want of sufficient capital, bad debts, and loss on trading through competition. The receiving order was made on their own petition in consequence of an execution being levied upon them by a trade creditor. The examination was eventually ordered to be closed.
