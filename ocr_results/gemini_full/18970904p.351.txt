LEITH.
There is nothing special calling for remark in regard to the arrivals this week, which consist of 2,019 pieces lumber per "Croft" (s), from New York, and 400 pit props per "Johannes," from Hudiksvall; 7,403 deals and battens per "Idog," from Sundsvall; 4 1/2 fathoms firewood per "Heimdal," from Risoer; and 50 1/2 fathoms firewood per "Julie," also from Risoer.

The end of the masons' strike and the general resumption of work is telling on the deliveries of wood from the docks and yards, and there is an active trade doing. Large arrivals of wood goods are expected this month both from Russia and Sweden, and things promise to be very busy at the docks. There has been no public sale this week, but one is announced for Tuesday, 7th inst.
