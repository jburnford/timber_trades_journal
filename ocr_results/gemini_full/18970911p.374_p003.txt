FAILURES AND ARRANGEMENTS.
[It must not be overlooked that in many cases creditors whose names are published in these lists hold security for their debts, sometimes for the full amount.]
ASHWORTH, J., builder, King Street, Manchester.—This debtor was publicly examined on Monday last at the Manchester Bankruptcy Court. The debtor's statement of affairs disclosed liabilities amounting to £1,991, and his assets were estimated to produce £696, thus leaving a deficiency of £1,295. Replying to questions the debtor stated that he attributed his failure to depreciation in the value of his house property. His assets represented the value of the equities in properties, and in his opinion they were worth that amount. He did not think they were mere paper assets. He had scheduled the properties, as he thought, reasonably. He first became aware of his insolvency in December last. He admitted that he had not applied for his discharge in a previous bankruptcy, and he could not say whether he received a release from his creditors when they received their dividend under that bankruptcy. He could not even say how much they received in the pound. He admitted that during the past three years he could not at any particular time say what his financial position was. He also admitted that he had not kept such books of account as were usual and proper in the business carried on by him. The examination was eventually ordered to be adjourned.
CROFT, JOHN EDMENTS, builder and contractor, Diss.—The statutory meeting of the creditors interested under this failure took place last week at the Crown Hotel, Ipswich. The summary of accounts filed by the debtor disclosed unsecured liabilities amounting to £354 13s. 10d., fully secured creditors £166, the value of the securities being estimated at £267, thus showing a surplus of £101. To partly secured creditors £52, the value of the securities being valued at £5. The gross liabilities were returned at £772 13s. 10d., of which £601 13s. 10d. was expected to rank against the estate for dividend. The assets consisted of stock-in-trade, furniture, horses, vehicles, &c., valued at £193, book debts, good, £141 17s., doubtful and bad debts £72 0s. 8d. The deficiency was estimated at £155 16s. 10d. As the debtor had no offer of composition to submit to his creditors it was resolved to adjudicate him bankrupt, and it was further resolved to appoint Mr. J. C. Pike, auctioneer, Diss, as trustee, to act in conjunction with a committee of inspection consisting of the following gentlemen:—T. Slack, R. Chenery, J. Flatman, J. P. Allbright, and J. C. Browne.
