Owing to the above arrivals the docks are excessively busy, and there is great pressure for quay space and storage ground. A portion of the arrivals is going into direct consumption, but as usual at this season of the year the bulk of the goods are being stored. Business continues active, and with a continuance of favourable weather a large consumption of wood goods may be reckoned upon.
