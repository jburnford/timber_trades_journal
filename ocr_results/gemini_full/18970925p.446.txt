446 THE TIMBER TRADES JOURNAL. SEPTEMBER 25, 1897.

GREENOCK.
The importations for the week are as follows :—"Luceria" (s), 1,260, from Bangor (Me.), discharged 120,918 bundles spoolwood for J. & P. Coats, Paisley, and 2,849 spruce deals and ends for James McLean & Co. ; the "Bravo" (s), 1,057, from Kaipara, New Zealand, brings full cargo of 276 logs sawn kauri pine, 132 logs hewn kauri pine, and 2,977 pieces kauri deals and boards, on account of Allison, Cousland, & Co.
This is the first cargo of kauri pine imported to Greenock. The "Concordia" (607), from Pensacola, discharged 3,622 logs sawn pitch pine, 629 logs sawn pitch pine deals, on account of J. M. Omsland, & Co. "Dunbeth" (1,680), from St. John, N.B., 141,289 bundles spoolwood, and 9,493 pieces spruce deals and ends for Robinson, Dunn, & Co. An auction sale will be held here by Messrs. Singleton, Dunn, & Co. on Thursday, 30th inst.

LEITH.
The arrivals of wood goods continue on a liberal scale, and include 358 pieces waney pine, 65 pieces white pine, 9 pieces oak, 20 pieces elm, 118 pieces birch, 38 pieces ash, 75 pieces hickory, per "Longhirst" (s), from Baltimore; 17,968 deals and battens, per "Marie," from Quebec; 2,983 deals and battens, per "Harold," from Skien; 13,455 deals and battens, per "Petersburg" (s), from Cronstadt; 44,540 oak staves and 1,254 fir deals, per "Dago" (s), from Libau; 22,534 deals and boards, per "Fremona" (s), from Montreal. The ship-ment per "Longhirst" (s), from Quebec, is for Park, Dobson, & Co., and is this firm's second entire steamer cargo from Quebec this season. Business continues brisk, and there is every appearance of a busy autumn in the arrivals trade. A noticeable feature in the arrivals is the large quantities of yellow pine and spruce deals from Canada, the latter being hitherto only sparingly imported to this market.
Pit props are reported somewhat weaker in price, owing to the large quantities offering, which seems strange in view of the enhanced freights, but that there will be any material back-come is very unlikely at this season of the year.
