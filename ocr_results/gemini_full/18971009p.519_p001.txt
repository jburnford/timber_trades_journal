although lower rates be anticipated. With the
prospects of a good harvest ensuing, we are,
however, hopeful of being shortly able to report
increased business in timber lines generally, and
have at the same time to record a higher range of
values for many of the leading descriptions.
The only counter is this market's consumption.
There is a considerable reduction in the export trade
which has been experienced during this year; nor at
present are there any indications of improve-
ment being shown in this direction, as ship-
ments to West Australia are now being received
direct from the main centres of supply. Some
little business has, however, lately been done
in this market to the colony just men-
tioned; but orders somewhat low figure.
Prices are based upon Swedish and Nor-
wegian goods and Oregon lumber are sufficient
to meet requirements for the next few months.
Meanwhile, as shipping rates for these as well as
other classes are ruling at high price, im-
porters are chary about entering into operations
of any magnitude.
