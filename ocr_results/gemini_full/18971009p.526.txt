LEITH.
As anticipated the arrivals this week have been numerous and important, consisting of 68,704 deals and battens, per "Toledo" (s), from Oravais and Wasa; 39,384 deals and battens, per "Peru," from Sundswall; 11,437 deals and battens per "airge," from Kragero; 4,819 battens, per "Karoline," from Kragero; 2,824 pcs. lathwood, and 314 rickers, per "Czar" (s), from St. Petersburg; 67,035 deals and battens, per "Vivienne" (s), from Kasho; 10,062 deals and battens, per "Otto," from Hernosand; 14,535 deals and battens, per "Titania," from Drammen; 14,665 deals and battens, per "Preciosa," from Rafso; 553 standard deals and battens, per "Peter Berg," from Fredrikstadt; 14,855 deals and battens, per "Franciseu," from G.fle; 5,854 deals and battens, and 17,821 pcs. latuwood, per "Moravia" (s), from Riga; 14,725 battens, per "Blanche," from Soderhamn; 15 logs walnut, 22 logs cherry, and 139 bundles lumber, per "Montana" (s), from Baltimore; 16,812 battens, per "Axel," from Abo; 83,979 oak staves and 4,051 deals, per "Ancona" (s), from Libau; 75 fathoms firewood, per "Eline," from Fredrikstad.
The steamer cargo of deals and battens, per "Toledo," is for Forslund & Rex, and that per "Vivienne" (s), for Garland & Roger, the other shipments being divided among

various importers.
With such a large importation the docks have been very busy, but the fine weather has greatly facilitated the work of discharging and piling, so that there has been no serious difficulty experienced in dealing with the goods.
Mr. D. W. Beattie held a public sale of deals and battens on Tuesday, 5th inst., at the Edinburgh Dock.
There was a good average attendance of buyers, and a considerable quantity of goods was disposed of, chiefly of the cheaper descriptions, which were apparently in greater demand than the high class Petersburg goods, owing to the advanced prices now required for such.
The high freight and insurance rates recently ruling are telling upon the costs of the goods now landing, and making merchants realise the necessity of holding firm.
