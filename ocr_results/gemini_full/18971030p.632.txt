GREENOCK.
The only importation at this port for the past week is a part cargo of 2,975 logs sawn pitch pine timber, per "Ramon de Larrinaga," from Pensacola, via Havre, and consigned to Messrs. Robinson, Dunn & Co.
We understand McDowall & Neilson will hold a public sale here on Thursday, the 4th prox.

LEITH.
The arrivals during the past week include a considerable number of sailers, and consist of 3,287 pine deals and battens per "Ganges" (s), from Montreal; 1,799 deals per "Acquire," from Arendal; 29,770 oak staves, 451 sleepers, and 2,624 planks per "Dwina" (s), from Libau; 8,453 deals and battens and 4,453 pieces lathwood per "Moidart" (s), from Riga; 34 fathoms firewood per "Ernst," from Arendal; 232,261 staves per "Hebe," from Kragero; 20,165 deals and battens per "Fannie," from Hernosand; 86 fathoms firewood per "Johnny," from Helsingfors; 8,150 battens per "Stjernen," from Drammen; 11,221 deals and battens per "Sirena," from Sundswall; 79 fathoms firewood per "Albatros," from Risor; 19,980 deals and battens per "King Oscar," from Nye Karleby; 844 deals per "Escalona" (s), from Montreal; 8,602 deals and battens per "Carl Rosenius," from Porsgrund.
Mr. D. W. Beattie held a public sale of deals and battens on Tuesday, the 26th inst., at the Edinburgh Dock. There was a limited attendance of buyers, and the quantity disposed of was correspondingly restricted. The goods offered included some fresh cargoes landing from Sundswall and Hernosand, but nothing like a clearance was effected, although prices for what was sold were well maintained.

D. W BEATTIE, TIMBER BROKER & SURVEYOR,
