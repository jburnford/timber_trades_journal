FAILURES AND ARRANGEMENTS.

BURGESS, ALFRED EDWARD.
merchant, Alsager.—This debtor made an application for his discharge through Mr. H. W. Adams, of Tunstall, at the last sitting of the Macclesfield Bankruptcy Court. The Official Receiver said this was one of the most extra-ordinary cases he had ever heard of. The debtor filed his petition about two years ago, and he then stated that he only attained his majority about six months before he filed his petition. He filed a statement of affairs show-ing liabilities amounting to £500, but only two creditors were able to prove. When the debtor intimated to him that he intended to apply for his discharge, he obtained a copy of the birth certificate from the district registrar, and to his great surprise he found that the debtor was not twenty-one until six months after the Receiving Order was made, the debtor being an "infant," he could not be made a bankrupt, and none of the creditors could prove against his estate under the bankruptcy proceedings. His Honour said it was indeed an extraordinary case, and he suspended the debtor's discharge for one day.
