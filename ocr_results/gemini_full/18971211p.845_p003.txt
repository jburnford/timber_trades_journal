GAZETTE.
FAILURES AND ARRANGEMENTS.
[It must not be overlooked that in many cases creditors whose names are published in these lists hold security for their debts, sometimes for the full amount.]

ATHERTON & DOLMAN, builders and contractors, Poplar, E.—The first meeting of creditors under this failure was held on Tuesday, before Mr. E. C. Hough, Official Receiver. The accounts were not filed, but the Chairman reported that according to a statement recently made out for the purposes of a private meeting the liabilities were returned at £10,548 and the assets at £6,631, thus showing a deficiency of £3,917. The partnership was apparently commenced in July, 1895, and the debtors had since had some extensive contracts. They ascribed their failure to losses in respect of such contracts. Mr. R. J. Ward, accountant, Clement's Inn, was appointed trustee to wind up the estate in bankruptcy, assisted by the following committee of inspection:—Mr. Robert Laking (Boulcott & Co.), Mr. Hooper (Doulton & Co.), and Mr. Archibald B. Dawney, managing director of D. away & Co. Limited. The public examination is fixed for January 12th.
