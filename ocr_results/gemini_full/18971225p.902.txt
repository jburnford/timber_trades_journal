GREENOCK.
Since last report a cargo of teak has arrived here, per barque "Collingwood" (s), from Rangoon, on account of Denny, Mott, & Dickson's Glasgow agency. The particulars are returned as 1,281 logs teak, and 482 planks.
No further auctions will take place here this year.

LEITH.
Since last report these are the following arrivals, viz. :—6,932 deals and boards, per "Escalona" (s), from Montreal; 24,976 deals and battens, and 22½ fathoms firewood, per "Livilight" (s), from Helsingfors; 437 bdls. poplar, per "Govino" from Baltimore; 3,308 deals per "Bellona" from Montreal, 32,186 staves and 163 bdls. poplar, per "Hazel Branch," (s), from Newport News.
There is no change to report in the state of trade this week, which continues to get into smaller compass as the year draws to a close.

FIRE IN GLASGOW.
About half-past twelve on Tuesday morning the Glasgow Fire Brigade were called to an out-break of fire on the premises of Andrew Campbell & Co., boatbuilders, Springfield Quay. Detachments from the Central and South, under Firemaster Paterson, were promptly on the scene, but the building was well alight before their arrival. Before the fire was checked considerable damage was done to the upper floors of the building, and to the saw-mill on the ground floor. The loss is estimated at from £1,000 to £1,200. The building is of brick, three storeys in height, and measures 90 ft. by 40 ft.
